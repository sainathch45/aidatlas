How it works

Two sides, one live match. Either can go first.

  1. 1
    A company posts standing demand
    how much crop residue they need, what price they're offering, which quality grades they'll take.
  2. 2
    A farmer lists stubble, by voice or text
    quantity, crop, village, when it's ready for pickup — speak it in Hindi or Punjabi, or type it.
  3. 3
    A live match and price appear instantly
    a real optimizer (not a guess) matches listings to demand and sets the price, the moment either side acts.
  4. 4
    Both sides confirm pickup
    the match is locked in; the company collects, the farmer gets paid instead of burning the field.
